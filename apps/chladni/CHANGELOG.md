# Chladni

## 0.2.0

- A large output is covered with the plate from edge to edge: Tiles, Small tiles or the one Plate, chosen with a press of E3. Neighbouring plates are turned over, so the figure runs on from one to the next.
- n and m go up to 24.
- The simplest figures have tidier lines.

## 0.1.1

- The knobs and keys are labelled on the device from the moment the App opens.

## 0.1.0

- First version: a humming plate with sand on it. n and m choose the figure and the pitch, the sand lets go and gathers again when they change, the plate goes from square to round, and a large output shows the same plate.
