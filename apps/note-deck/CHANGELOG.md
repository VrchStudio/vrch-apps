# Note Deck

## 0.1.0

Play 25 consecutive scale notes on a 5×5 grid or switch to 16 drum pads. Use Connect USB to get playing, shape pad velocity by touch, and switch between Macros 1–4 and 5–8. The encoders and performance keys stay active while changing scale.

Turn on Local sound to play through the device speaker, on its own or alongside USB MIDI.
