# Hit Shapes

## 0.4.0

- Tune and Decay now change the whole picture: it leans as the voices are tuned up or down, and longer decays make every trace larger and longer-lived.
- Hold K3 for the next colors.
- Orbit shows each pattern as its own picture: every step the kick plays casts a ray across the screen, and the trails are already there before Start.

## 0.3.0

- A kick now shoves the shapes around it aside for a moment, in every style.
- Score moves on from bar to bar too: over eight bars its lanes close into one line of larger signs and open again.

## 0.2.0

- Three styles of picture to change between at any time: Score, Orbit and Weave. Hold K4 or tap the style button.
- The picture now moves on from bar to bar: in Orbit traces ride their rings and the dial opens and closes over sixteen bars; in Weave each bar becomes a row that climbs the screen.
- Colors moved to a button on the picture.

## 0.1.1

- Keep every shape in its pure color: a trace now shrinks away at the end of its bar instead of dimming.
- Show the bar's pace in the picture: the beat being played sits on a slightly lifted ground.
- Draw each shape closer to the moment its hit is heard.

## 0.1.0

- A drum machine where every hit has a shape: eight voices on 16 steps draw the rhythm as a picture, on the device and on a big screen.
