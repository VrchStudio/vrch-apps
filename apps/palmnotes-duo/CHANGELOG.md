# PalmNotes Duo

## 0.2.1

- Show the camera picture sharp on a large output or second screen: it now arrives at the camera's own size instead of a small, blurry copy.

## 0.2.0

- Show your camera picture and both hands on a large output or second screen; the note you play lights up in its colour.

## 0.1.4

- Ask you to allow sound and press K2 again when the browser has not started sound, instead of pointing to the output settings.
- Keep routine tracking messages out of the browser console's errors.

## 0.1.3

- Download about 11 MB less; hand tracking needs a browser with WebAssembly SIMD (current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
- Keep camera frames and usage data on this device: the tracker no longer contacts any outside service.

## 0.1.2

- Play with your hands in a browser that allows the camera, such as the Playground; without a camera, Preview still auditions notes.

## 0.1.1

- Add a VRCH-style vector hand-and-notes icon for Launcher, My Apps and Guide.
- Keep the existing audio, hand tracking and saved settings unchanged.

## 0.1.0

- Publish the original installed package as the update baseline.
