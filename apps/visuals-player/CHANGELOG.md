# Visuals Player

## 0.3.0

- Turn the camera on or off with the camera button beside mute on the device screen.

## 0.2.0

- Mute or unmute the music with the speaker button on the device screen; the sound fades out and back in, and the picture keeps the beat.
- Starting the music and switching to Silent or Mic / Line in fade the sound instead of cutting it.

## 0.1.6

- Show the camera picture sharp on a large output or second screen: it now arrives at the camera's own size instead of a small, blurry copy.

## 0.1.5

- Start sooner in the Playground: one download, and the picture plays as soon as its first files arrive.

## 0.1.4

- Start in the Playground after about 5 MB: the songs and camera tracking keep loading while the picture plays.
- Keep routine tracking messages out of the browser console's errors.

## 0.1.3

- Curated visuals that move with four original songs, your microphone or line in, or play silently.
- Control it like a Visual in Live Console: E1–E4 shape the picture in pages, K3 opens Visuals and Visual Settings, K4 opens the Player for sound, track, auto change and camera.
- Auto change picks the next visual on a new section of the song; playing a visual yourself holds it.
- Turn on the camera to steer visuals with your hands and see image visuals use your picture.
- On a device with a second screen, the picture fills that screen while the touch screen keeps the controls.
