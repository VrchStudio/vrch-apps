# Visuals Player

## 0.6.0

- Play your works from My Visuals under My Library, each by itself even when two use the same visual.
- Knob and Visual Settings changes to a work stay in Visuals Player; the work in My Visuals is unchanged.
- A large output or second screen shows the exact version of the work that is playing.

## 0.5.2

- The built-in music plays as MP3, so muting and the device volume work on iPhone too.
- One tap starts the music on iPhone, even while a track is still downloading on a slow connection.

## 0.5.1

- Where the browser needs a tap before it plays sound, the speaker shows as muted until then, and a tap on it starts the music.

## 0.5.0

- The built-in music is six tracks by independent artists from Free Music Archive, credited in Player and the Guide.
- The picture follows the built-in tracks from their own sound, with the music playing, muted or Silent.

## 0.4.1

- Takes the current visual catalog from VRCH Pages; nothing changes on screen.

## 0.4.0

- The built-in music is four original songs made in the App, from deep house to lo-fi, with drums from Drum 808.
- With the camera off, image visuals show landscapes, light and city scenes; the pictures of people are gone.
- Visuals open with new previews.
- The App is about half the size.

## 0.3.14

- Player shows the Mic / Line in level beside Sound, and No signal when the input stays quiet.
- The device screen briefly names the next visual or track when they change by themselves.
- Holding K3 fills its label until Visual Settings opens.

## 0.3.13

- With Mic / Line in, short hits such as drums reach the picture even when it draws fewer frames than the screen refreshes.

## 0.3.12

- With the picture on a second screen, Visuals stays open while you try visuals there; Done closes it.

## 0.3.11

- In Visuals, a tap on a visual plays it at once; turning E1 still moves through the cards and pressing E1 plays the one chosen.

## 0.3.10

- On a small host with a second screen, the picture there runs smoother and follows Mic / Line in more closely; the device screen shows a lighter preview while it is on.

## 0.3.9

- On the device, Visuals Player also opens playing music when Mic / Line in was chosen last time.

## 0.3.8

- Music now really starts by itself when Visuals Player opens; in a browser it starts with the first tap anywhere on the page.

## 0.3.7

- Music plays as soon as Visuals Player opens, unmuted; a browser that waits for a first tap starts it on that tap.

## 0.3.6

- On the device screen, a finger tap on or near the camera and speaker buttons turns them on and off; it no longer opens Player.

## 0.3.5

- The camera button shows a camera crossed by a slash when the camera is off.

## 0.3.4

- Show every knob name whole and different on each page, also with a 2x or 4x mark.
- Share links now open with the same visual, knob values and track.
- In a browser, the camera and Mic / Line in open only when you turn them on.

## 0.3.3

- Show the camera at once the first time you turn it on in a visual that uses pictures; it no longer stays black until the camera is turned off and on again.

## 0.3.2

- Install Visuals Player from Official Apps on the device: the release package now matches the package the device checks.

## 0.3.1

- Keep the speaker button on the device screen with every sound choice: with Silent or Mic / Line in it shows as muted, and a tap plays the music.
- Mark the camera button with a small cross while the camera is off, and colour both buttons the same way: pink while the music plays or the camera is on.

## 0.3.0

- Turn the camera on or off with the camera button beside mute on the device screen.

## 0.2.0

- Mute or unmute the music with the speaker button on the device screen; the sound fades out and back in, and the picture keeps the beat.
- Starting the music and switching to Silent or Mic / Line in fade the sound instead of cutting it.

## 0.1.6

- Show the camera picture sharp on a large output or second screen: it now arrives at the camera's own size instead of a small, blurry copy.

## 0.1.5

- Start sooner in the Playground: one download, and the picture plays as soon as its first files arrive.

## 0.1.4

- Start in the Playground after about 5 MB: the songs and camera tracking keep loading while the picture plays.
- Keep routine tracking messages out of the browser console's errors.

## 0.1.3

- Curated visuals that move with four original songs, your microphone or line in, or play silently.
- Control it like a Visual in Live Console: E1–E4 shape the picture in pages, K3 opens Visuals and Visual Settings, K4 opens the Player for sound, track, auto change and camera.
- Auto change picks the next visual on a new section of the song; playing a visual yourself holds it.
- Turn on the camera to steer visuals with your hands and see image visuals use your picture.
- On a device with a second screen, the picture fills that screen while the touch screen keeps the controls.
