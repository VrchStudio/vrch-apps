# Shader Editor

## 0.3.0

- Open your works from My Visuals under My Library, each by itself even when two use the same visual.
- Update Visual saves a new version of your own work in My Visuals; Save Visual on any other work there saves your copy.
- A large output or second screen shows the exact version of the work you are editing.
- Without My Visuals access, the App keeps its own saved visuals as before.

## 0.2.3

- Every visual has a new preview, drawn the same way as in Visuals Player; none shows a person.

## 0.2.2

- Show a camera or image source at once when its size changes, such as the camera's first picture; it no longer stays black.

## 0.2.1

- Show the camera picture sharp on a large output or second screen: it now arrives at the camera's own size instead of a small, blurry copy.

## 0.2.0

- Show the visual you are editing on a large output or second screen, with its sound, tracking and image sources.
- Use the current shared visual engine and controls.

## 0.1.3

- Keep routine tracking messages out of the browser console's errors.

## 0.1.2

- Download about 11 MB less; tracking needs a browser with WebAssembly SIMD (current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
- Keep camera frames and usage data on this device: the tracker no longer contacts any outside service.

## 0.1.1

- Add a VRCH-style vector shader icon.
- Keep audio analysis on a silent output while preserving visual presets and saved workspaces.

## 0.1.0
