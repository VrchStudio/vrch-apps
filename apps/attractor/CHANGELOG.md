# Attractor

## 0.4.0

- Two pieces of music come with the App, Strange Loop and Sacred Orbit: hold K4 and the figure moves with the one playing. Hold again for the next piece, then the audio input, then off.
- The soft light around a figure no longer shows as steps.
- On the device a figure at rest is drawn at full sharpness again, however coarsely it was drawn while it moved.

## 0.3.1

- The device now really draws fewer pixels when frames come late (0.3.0 never did).

## 0.3.0

- Every figure can be turned in space: drag the picture, flick it to keep it spinning, or hold K3.
- Listen follows quiet, beatless sound too: low, middle and high parts each move a letter.
- Lorenz, Aizawa and Thomas now rest and sharpen like the others when nothing moves.
- The formula on the device screen sits on a small label.
- Smoother motion on the device: it draws fewer pixels when frames come late.

## 0.2.0

- Five more formulas: Gumowski-Mira and Ikeda, and Lorenz, Aizawa and Thomas, which turn slowly in three dimensions.
- Hold K4 and the figure follows the audio input: bass pushes it and each beat nudges it.
- Hold K2 to hear the formula.
- K2 and K4 now act when the key is let go, as K3 does.

## 0.1.0

- First version: four two-line formulas drawn as silk from millions of points, with the four knobs as the letters a, b, c and d, on the device and on a large output.
