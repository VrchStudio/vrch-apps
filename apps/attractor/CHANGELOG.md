# Attractor

## 0.7.0

- A large output can show the figure in four ways, chosen under Output in Figures: Whole, Three (the same figure from two other sides beside it), Mirror (the figure and its mirror image) or Close (filling the screen from edge to edge).

## 0.6.0

- No key needs holding any more. K2 is the next figure, K3 opens Figures (formula, preset, colours, text), K4 or a tap on the picture opens Player.
- The show has three settings in Player: Auto, Hold and Still. Turning a knob or choosing a figure during Auto holds the show, so it never changes what you have just set.
- Figures and Player look and work like the panels in Visuals Player.
- A Still figure rests and sharpens even while the music plays.

## 0.5.0

- The App opens in a show: the figure turns, tilts and sways by itself, moves with the music, and goes on to new figures, formulas and colours where the music changes. Hold K3 to stop it and rest.
- K4 opens Player: music, microphone or line in, or silence; the piece; the show. A speaker on the picture mutes the music and brings it back.
- The figure follows the music from the first moment, also while muted.
- Lorenz, Aizawa and Thomas are drawn as one long line wound into the figure.
- A large output fills its height, and in the show the figure grows past the edges and comes back.
- K2 held is now the next formula. The formula is no longer played as sound.

## 0.4.1

- The soft light around a figure ends evenly, without a faint blocky fringe.

## 0.4.0

- Two pieces of music come with the App, Strange Loop and Sacred Orbit: hold K4 and the figure moves with the one playing. Hold again for the next piece, then the audio input, then off.
- The soft light around a figure no longer shows as steps.
- On the device a figure at rest is drawn at full sharpness again, however coarsely it was drawn while it moved.

## 0.3.1

- The device now really draws fewer pixels when frames come late (0.3.0 never did).

## 0.3.0

- Every figure can be turned in space: drag the picture, flick it to keep it spinning, or hold K3.
- Listen follows quiet, beatless sound too: low, middle and high parts each move a letter.
- Lorenz, Aizawa and Thomas now rest and sharpen like the others when nothing moves.
- The formula on the device screen sits on a small label.
- Smoother motion on the device: it draws fewer pixels when frames come late.

## 0.2.0

- Five more formulas: Gumowski-Mira and Ikeda, and Lorenz, Aizawa and Thomas, which turn slowly in three dimensions.
- Hold K4 and the figure follows the audio input: bass pushes it and each beat nudges it.
- Hold K2 to hear the formula.
- K2 and K4 now act when the key is let go, as K3 does.

## 0.1.0

- First version: four two-line formulas drawn as silk from millions of points, with the four knobs as the letters a, b, c and d, on the device and on a large output.
