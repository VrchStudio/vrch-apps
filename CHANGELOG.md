# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added

- Add Chladni: a humming plate with sand on it. Two knobs choose the note, and the sand gathers into that note's figure, on the device and on a large output.
- Attractor: six presets for every formula and six sets of colours, with Aurora and Flare new.
- Attractor: a large output can show the figure whole, in three views or mirrored.
- Attractor: choose formula, preset and colours in a Figures panel, and Auto, Hold or Still in Player; no key needs holding.
- Attractor: opens in a show that performs by itself with the music; Player and a speaker button choose and mute the sound; Lorenz, Aizawa and Thomas are drawn as one line.
- Attractor: two pieces of music come with the App and move the figure while they play; an audio input can drive it instead.
- Attractor: turn every figure in space by dragging or let it spin, on the device and on a large output.
- Attractor: five more formulas, three of them turning in three dimensions, a figure that can follow the audio input, and the formula played as sound.
- Add Attractor: four two-line formulas drawn as silk from millions of points, with the four knobs as the letters a, b, c and d, on the device and on a large output.
- Add XY Scope, an oscilloscope synthesizer: the left and right channels draw the figure you hear, on the device and on a large output, and an input mode shows any incoming sound as a wave, XY figure or spectrum with a tuner.
- Add Botanica Core, a petalcore synthesizer around a holographic glass flower that also plays on a large output or second screen.
- Add Hit Shapes, a drum machine where every hit has a shape and the rhythm becomes a picture on a large output or second screen.
- Group Botanica Core, Disco Ball, Magic Circle and Body Vibes under Audiovisual in the Playground, second after Visuals.
- Let the vrch.io Playground list, group and load official Apps directly from GitHub.
- Add Visuals Player: curated visuals that move with original songs, your microphone or line in, featured first in the catalog.
- Show Disco Ball and Magic Circle on a large output or second screen, and mark Apps with an output in the Playground catalog.
- Show Shader Editor, Shaker Shaker and Vape Grain on a large output or second screen.
- Show Body Vibes, PalmNotes Duo and Wavescape on a large output or second screen.
- Mute Visuals Player from a speaker button on the device screen, with the sound fading out and back in.
- Turn the Visuals Player camera on or off from a button beside mute.
